Welcome to BlogAPI

Discover articles written by members of our community, interact with published content, and share your own ideas with the world.

Whether you are a reader looking for interesting content or an author wanting to publish blogs, BlogAPI provides a simple platform to connect writers and readers.

For Readers

  • Browse published blog posts
  • View author profiles
  • Read articles from different writers
  • Follow your favorite content
Explore Published Posts

For Authors

  • Create and manage blog posts
  • Save drafts before publishing
  • Publish content for readers
  • Manage your profile and posts
Login to access Author Dashboard

Getting Started

New to BlogAPI?

  1. Create an account.
  2. Login to access your dashboard.
  3. Choose whether to read content or become an author.
  4. Start exploring or publishing posts.

BlogAPI Project

Built with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Node.js, Express, PostgreSQL, JWT Authentication, and Axios.
